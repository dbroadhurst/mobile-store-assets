mobile-store-assets — phone layouts · portrait screenshots

Portrait phone frame · portrait screenshot canvas (1290 × 2796) · for portrait-only apps. 8 layouts · every text zone sits on a computed-alpha container · palette shown is a placeholder (deep indigo → teal); the production palette is derived per theme.
Phone · portrait shots Phone · landscape shots Tablet · portrait shots Tablet · landscape shots

Container styles

solid-panel rounded rect, ~0.78 alpha, drop shadow — hero, key headlines.
soft-band full-width band with feathered edge, ~0.55 alpha — captions, banners.
chip small filled pill — badges, feature claims, callouts.

Scrim math

Container alpha is solved so that contrast(text, blended-bg) ≥ target.
Headlines aim for 4.5:1 (AAA-equivalent for large text). Body & captions aim for 4.5:1 (WCAG AA).

Merge rule

Two zones whose padded bboxes overlap (or sit within 0.6 × line-height of each other) are merged into a single container — no clashing rectangles when headline + subhead are close.

Auto-remediation

Render → gate samples actual pixels under each zone → any failure escalates that container's target ratio and re-paints. Up to 2 retries before the CLI exits non-zero.

Copy budget (Apptweak + Adapty, 2025)

Headlines aim for ≤ 7 words (Adapty: "under one sentence per image"). Benefit-led framing ("Plan your week in minutes") outperforms generic labels ("Calendar app") — Apptweak. Decision happens in 17–50 ms: the first 3 screenshots carry conversion.

Safe-area rule

Every text run is fit-tested against its container's width (container.width − 2 × padding). Lines that don't fit are auto-shrunk or wrapped at render time. Mockups below use the worst-case padding so they read as the production output will.

Story arc — sequencing across 10 slots

Per StoreMaven / SplitMetrics, ~90% of decisions are made on slots 1–3 (iOS search shows the first three portrait shots inline). Treat them as a sentence: Value → Flow → Trust. Slots 4–8 carry feature depth; slot 10 closes with a CTA. This sequence is what story-arc.ts will assign by default, and what design/layouts-phone-portrait-shots.html documents:

The 25% zoom test — what users see in search

iOS search results show portrait screenshots at roughly 75 px wide — about a quarter of authored size. If a layout's headline isn't readable here, it isn't doing any conversion work. The thumbnails below are the same SVGs as the full mockups, scaled to thumbnail size.

01
Hero Type
Slot 1 · Value  /  Slot 10 · CTA

Brand icon → big stacked benefit headline → subhead → row of honest feature chips. No phone — the screenshot promises the outcome, not the UI. Owns slot 1 (the highest-leverage A/B test target) and reprises as slot 10 with a CTA variant.

  • Containers: solid-panel merged (headline + subhead) · chip × N feature pills
  • Roles: headline (3 lines, accent on line 3), subheadline (≤ 2 lines), chip (≤ 4)
  • Use case: closing screenshot · feature graphic value slot
02
Caption Band
Slot 2 · Flow  /  Slot 8 · Feature

Phone hero in the top 70%, full-width soft-band caption at the bottom. The most "App Store native" composition — used by every Apple-made app. Verb-led caption ("Three taps to track…") shows how the value happens, not what it's called.

  • Containers: soft-band bottom 28% (feathered top edge)
  • Roles: headline (≤ 2 lines), subheadline (≤ 2 lines, optional)
  • Use case: feature screenshots · the workhorse default
03
Headline Masthead
Slot 1 alt · Value  /  Slot 9 · Trust

Solid headline panel up top (Adapty: "text in the upper half" for the Value slot) with an eyebrow chip, full phone occupying the bottom. Reads strongest at thumbnail size — the headline lives in the part of the image that thumbnail-cropping always preserves.

  • Containers: solid-panel top + chip eyebrow
  • Roles: badge/eyebrow (1), headline (≤ 3 lines, accent on last)
  • Use case: intro / value slot · "What's new" framing
04
Editorial Card
Slot 2 alt · Flow  /  Slot 4 · Feature

Phone is the dominant hero (full width, slight tilt, clipping the top edge). A solid panel anchors to the bottom-left with a vertical accent rule and overlaps the phone — magazine feel without column-squeeze. The "STEP n/N" chip turns one screen into part of a clear sequence.

  • Containers: solid-panel bottom-left (merged step + headline + body) + chip step indicator
  • Roles: badge (step), headline (≤ 3 lines, accent on last), body (≤ 2 lines)
  • Use case: step-by-step screenshots · feature walkthroughs · "how it works" sequences
05
Vertical Bento
Slot 3 · Trust  /  Slot 5 · Feature

Three vertical bands: headline strip on top, full-width phone hero in the middle, two equal stat tiles at the bottom. Stat tiles ("3s per entry", "100% on device") are the trust slot's most efficient proof — concrete numbers in branded chips, no testimonials needed.

  • Containers: solid-panel top headline · phone card (white tile) · 2 × solid-panel stat tiles (one inverted in accent)
  • Roles: headline (≤ 2 lines) · 2 × (label + stat + caption)
  • Use case: feature-rich apps · stat-driven proof · "by the numbers"
06
Feature Stack
Slot 3 · Trust  /  Slot 9 · Recap

No phone — the screenshot itself is the feature list. Icon + headline anchor the top; four full-width numbered rows fill the body (the fourth highlighted in the accent colour); a credibility chip closes. This is the layout 1Password, Bear, Day One, Pocket use for "what's inside" — and it converts highly when used as the slot-3 trust beat.

  • Containers: solid-panel title · 4 × solid-panel feature rows (last in accent) · chip footer
  • Roles: headline · 4 × (badge + label + body) · footer caption
  • Use case: feature lists · "what's inside" · "how it works" steps
07
Dual Phone
Slot 6 · Feature

Two phones tilted toward each other showing different states (light/dark, A/B, before/after). Headline panel on top, two chips below. Use when the feature genuinely needs a comparison — A/B or theming. Don't use it just to "show two phones".

  • Containers: solid-panel top (merged headline + subhead) + 2 × chip footer
  • Roles: headline (≤ 2 lines), subheadline, 2 chips
  • Use case: "before / after" · light/dark themes · A/B variants
08
Tilted Card
Slot 7 · Feature (premium)

Phone presented inside a tilted floating card (gives a "product photo" feel — drop shadow + slight rotation), with a solid headline panel anchored at the bottom. Use for the most polished / brand-forward feature; works best when the phone screen itself is visually strong.

  • Containers: phone in card · solid-panel bottom (merged headline + subhead)
  • Roles: headline (≤ 2 lines, accent on line 2), subheadline (≤ 2 lines)
  • Use case: brand-forward / premium feel · device-as-hero shots
